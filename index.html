REFERENCE MANUAL

PURPOSE

AI generates salience, not truth. It is a "confident autocomplete" that provides what sounds right, not necessarily what is true.

NORTH STAR: AI dependence erodes depth of understanding. Use it to amplify inquiry, not bypass it.
1.0 SYSTEM ETHICS

Privacy

Never share personal data or sensitive information.

Copyright

Don't input protected content you don't own.

Labor & Environment

Use prompts efficiently to reduce resource use.

Bias

Assume outputs reflect training biases and verify.

2.0 THE AI CYCLE LOOP

01 PREPARE: Goal: Decision, learning, or production.

02 PROMPT: Formula: Persona + Context + Constraints.

03 VERIFY: Test claims, don't trust tone.

04 REFLECT: Risks: Bias, hallucination, over-reliance.

3.0 ADVANCED PROTOCOLS

Protocol 1: Visible Checkpoints

Request: List assumptions, show calculations, identify failure modes.

Protocol 2: Adversarial Playoff

Persona 1 (Optimist) vs Persona 2 (Skeptic). Persona 3 (Judge) summarizes.

AI USE CATEGORIES

[CAT 1] No AI allowed (Exams, confidential records).

[CAT 2] Allowed with acknowledgment (Research, brainstorming).

[CAT 3] Required—verify critically (Translation, summaries).
